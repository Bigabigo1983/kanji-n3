規制
kisei
regulation / restriction
規模
kibo
scale / size
ポイント · Notes
規則（きそく） is a rule people must follow, such as school rules. 規制（きせい） is a restriction set by an authority, for example on traffic or business. 規模（きぼ） is different: it means the scale or size of something.
