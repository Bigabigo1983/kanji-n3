STROKE ORDER
音読み · On reading
ソウ (sou)
単語 · Word
競争
kyousou
competition
例文 · Example
その会社は海外の企業と競争しています。
Sono kaisha wa kaigai no kigyou to kyousou shite imasu.
That company competes with overseas companies.
訓読み · Kun reading
あらそ (araso)
単語 · Word
争う
arasou
compete / dispute
例文 · Example
二つのチームが優勝を争っています。
Futatsu no chiimu ga yuushou wo arasotte imasu.
Two teams are competing for the championship.
ポイント · Notes
競争（きょうそう） is competition, for example between companies or players. 争う（あらそう） is broader: it can mean competing for something, as in ～を争う in the example, but also arguing or fighting over something in a dispute.
