ポイント · Notes
資金, 資料 and 資格 all use シ, but their meanings are very different. 資金（しきん） is money or funds for a purpose, 資料（しりょう） is materials or data, and 資格（しかく） is a qualification or eligibility. Learn each as a whole word.
